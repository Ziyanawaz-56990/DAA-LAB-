[Fractional Knapsack with Deterioration Rate] You have n items with base values v_i, weights w_i, and decay rates lambda_i > 0. If an item is consumed at time t, its effective value density decays to (v_i/w_i) - lambda_i*t. Knapsack capacity is W. Determine the optimal scheduling order and fractional choices to maximise total value. By choosing the proper input representation, write a program in C to validate your algorithm and derive the complexity analysis of your algorithm.
