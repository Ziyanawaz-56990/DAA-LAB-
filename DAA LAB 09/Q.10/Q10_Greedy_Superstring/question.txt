[Greedy Superstring Conjecture] (Open Unsolved Problem): Given a set of n strings S = {s_1, ..., s_n}, find the shortest single string that contains every s_i as a substring. You are advised to explore more on this problem to develop an insight.

Background: The conjecture, formulated nearly four decades ago, hypothesised that the classic greedy algorithm (repeatedly merging the pair with the maximum overlap) always yields a superstring no more than twice the length of the optimal solution (2-approximation).
A September 2026 paper titled "Disproving the Greedy Superstring Conjecture" published on arXiv demonstrated that the approximation ratio for instances where input strings have an even length k >= 10 is at least (9k+2)/(4k+4), establishing a limit of 9/4 (2.25) as k -> infinity. This disproves the long-standing 2-approximation claim. The announcement generated excitement among theorists, especially because the counterexample was found with AI assistance. However, the claim of disproof has not been officially validated yet.
