Question 3: [Bidirectional BFS for Shortest Transformation Sequence in a Word Graph]

You are given:
  - A dictionary D containing N unique words, where every word has the exact
    same length L.
  - A starting word A and a target word B, both of length L.

A valid mutation step consists of changing exactly one character of a word such
that the resulting word exists in dictionary D. A transformation sequence from
A to B is an ordered sequence of words W0, W1, W2, ..., Wk such that:
  1. W0 = A and Wk = B.
  2. Every intermediate word Wi is in D for all 1 <= i <= k. (Note: whether A
     must initially be in D is typically specified by the input constraints,
     but B must always be in D.)
  3. For every adjacent pair (W(i-1), Wi), the two words differ by exactly one
     character (i.e., their Hamming distance is 1).

Objective: Compute the minimum number of mutations k required to transform A
into B. If A cannot be transformed into B through valid intermediate words in D,
return -1 (or report that no valid path exists). Constraints:
  - N <= 10^5 (number of dictionary words)
  - L <= 10   (length of each word)
  - All words consist solely of lowercase English letters ('a' to 'z').

By choosing the proper input representation, write a program in C to validate
your algorithm and derive the complexity analysis of your algorithm.
